1. What Shop Manager does

Shop Manager watches how shoppers use your Shopify store, tells you what is getting in their way, ranks the problems by what they are likely costing you, and tracks whether the fixes you make work. On the Done for you plan, our team also designs and runs tests with you and builds the fixes on your store.
