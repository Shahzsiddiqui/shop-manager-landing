DPA

Data processing addendum

Last updated: 29 October 2026

When you use Shop Manager, you are the controller of your shoppers' personal data and Sirge is your processor. This page summarises the data processing addendum (DPA) that governs that. It is part of our terms for every store.

Need the signed DPA? We sign it with every store that asks, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK addendum for international transfers. Book a call or email shahz@sirge.io and we'll send it for signature.

What we process

  • Whose data: people who visit your store's pages, and customers whose orders Shopify sends us.
  • What data: recordings of how pages are used (with everything typed masked and checkout never recorded), visit events, a random visitor id, and order details (order number, value, products, time).
  • Why: only to show you where shoppers get stuck, what it costs, and whether your fixes worked.
  • How long: recordings 90 days; everything else while you use Shop Manager, then deleted.

What we commit to

  • Your instructions only. We process your shoppers' data only to provide Shop Manager to you, never for our own purposes, advertising or sale.
  • Confidentiality. Everyone who can access the data is bound to keep it confidential, and access is limited to the people who need it to run the service.
  • Security. Encryption in transit and at rest, masking in the shopper's browser, access control and automatic deletion — see our security page.
  • Sub-processors. We use only the companies on our sub-processors page, under written terms at least as protective as these. We tell you before adding a new one, and you can object.
  • Breaches. We tell you without undue delay, and within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting your data, with what we know and what we're doing.
  • Helping you. We help you answer shoppers' requests to see or delete their data — Shopify's erasure requests reach us and are handled automatically — and with impact assessments where you need them.
  • Deletion at the end. When you leave, we delete your store's data, including recordings and backups as they cycle out.
  • Proof. We answer reasonable questions about how we protect your data and give you the information you need to show you comply.

International transfers

Shop Manager stores and processes data in the United States. For shoppers in the EU, UK or Switzerland, transfers are covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK addendum) included in the signed DPA.

Your part as controller

  • Tell your shoppers about Shop Manager in your privacy policy — the install guide has a paragraph to copy.
  • Keep Shopify's cookie consent banner on wherever the law requires consent for analytics. Shop Manager follows it automatically.
  • Tell us if your store sells to children or sells age-restricted products, so we can agree how it is set up.
