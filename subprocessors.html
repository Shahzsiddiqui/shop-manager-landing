Sub-processors

Last updated: 29 October 2026

These are the companies that process personal data for Shop Manager. Each one works under written terms that protect the data at least as well as our data processing addendum. We tell merchants before we add a new one.

Amazon Web Services (AWS)

Runs the app and stores recordings, visit events and the weekly data, and sends our emails (Amazon SES).

United States — Oregon (us-west-2)

Supabase

The app's database (stores, accounts, the problems we found and their summaries) and sign-in.

United States (Oregon)

Anthropic

The weekly analysis: AI reads masked summaries of how shoppers moved through the store and the problems we found, and drafts the write-ups we review before you see them. Recordings themselves are not sent.

United States

Shopify

Your store's platform: installing the app, sending us orders, the customer privacy (cookie consent) settings Shop Manager follows, and shoppers' erasure requests.

Global — under your own agreement with Shopify

Email is sent only through Amazon SES; we don't use any other email provider. This website loads no third-party analytics or advertising tools.
