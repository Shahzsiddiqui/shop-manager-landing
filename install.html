Install Shop Manager

Setup takes a few steps and no developer. We do it with you on a call if you like.

  1. Book a call and get your store set up

    Book a call. We look at your store together, create your Shop Manager account, and send you a sign-in link and your store's snippet.

  2. Add the snippet (or the app)

    In Shopify, go to Online Store → Themes → … → Edit code. Add the snippet we send you as snippets/sirge-tracking.liquid, then add {% render 'sirge-tracking' %} just before </head> in layout/theme.liquid. If you install the Shop Manager app instead, turn on its app embeds under Customize → App embeds.

    The snippet loads after your page is ready, never blocks it, and runs only on your storefront — Shopify's checkout never loads it.

  3. Set up consent

    In Shopify, go to Settings → Customer privacy and turn on the cookie banner for the regions where the law asks for consent (for example the EU and UK). Shop Manager follows it automatically: no recording until a shopper agrees, and it stops if they change their mind.

    Then add this paragraph to your store's privacy policy (Settings → Policies):

    We use Shop Manager, a service by Sirge, to understand how visitors use our store so we can fix problems and make it easier to shop. Shop Manager records how our pages are used — such as pages visited, scrolls and clicks — with anything you type masked, and it never records checkout or payment details. It works on our behalf as a data processor, deletes recordings after 90 days and does not sell your data. Where the law requires it, it only runs after you agree to analytics cookies. Learn more at https://shop.sirge.io/privacy.

  4. Open your staff link

    So your team's own visits don't count, open your store with ?sirge_staff=1 on the end — for example https://yourstore.com/?sirge_staff=1 — once in every browser your team uses (phone, laptop, Instagram's browser). It lasts a year. ?sirge_staff=0 turns it off.

  5. Mask anything else private

    Typed inputs are masked already. If a page shows something else private — a loyalty number, a gift message — add data-sirge-mask to that element and it is hidden in recordings.

  6. Check it's working

    Log in. Within an hour of your first visitors, your home page shows recordings coming in. Your first Monday email arrives once there is a week of visits.
