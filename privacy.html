Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Shop Manager is a pre-launch product (working title).

This page describes what we collect on this website, why, and what we do with it. The short version: we collect what you type into our forms, standard analytics about how the page is used, and nothing else. We don't sell your data, ever.

What we collect

  • What you give us: your email address and, if you choose, your store URL — when you request early access, an audit, a call, or the weekly list.
  • How you found us: standard campaign parameters (UTM tags), the referring site, and the page you landed on.
  • How the page is used: anonymous interaction signals on this page (for example scroll depth, clicks, and the on-page demo events), and — if you answer it — the one-tap exit question about what we missed.

What we use it for

  • To contact you about early access, your audit, or your call — the thing you asked for.
  • To understand which channels and messages bring the right visitors, so a small team can spend its budget sensibly.
  • To improve this page. The live demo on the landing page runs in your browser; its session log is shown to you and isn't transmitted unless you submit a form.

Analytics and advertising tools

When enabled, this site uses common third-party tools that set cookies or similar identifiers: Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google tag (GA4/Ads), Microsoft Clarity, and Cloudflare Web Analytics. These help us measure whether our ads worked and how the page performs. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy. If you use an ad blocker or your browser blocks these, the page works fine without them.

Where your data lives

Form submissions are stored in a private spreadsheet we control (Google Sheets, via Google Apps Script). Access is limited to the founder(s). We keep signups until launch onboarding is complete or you ask us to delete yours.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your personal data.
  • Send your data to anyone except the service providers listed above.
  • Sign you up for anything you didn't ask for.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe: every email we send has an opt-out, or just reply "stop."
  • Access or deletion: email us and we'll show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it — usually within a few days.
  • Cookies: you can block or clear cookies in your browser at any time.

This policy covers this pre-launch website only. When the Shop Manager product launches (watching sessions on merchants' own stores), it will ship with its own privacy policy and data-processing terms covering that service.
