Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Shop Manager is a service by Sirge that watches how shoppers use Shopify stores and tells the store what to fix. This policy covers two groups of people: merchants and visitors to this website, and shoppers on the stores that use Shop Manager.

The short version. We record how pages are used, not who is using them. What shoppers type is masked in their browser, checkout is never recorded, recordings are deleted after 90 days, we follow each store's cookie consent settings, and we never sell or share personal data.

1. Merchants and visitors to this website

For this website and your Shop Manager account, Sirge decides how data is used (we are the controller).

  • If you book a call or email us: your name, email address, your store's address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it to reply and to set up your account.
  • Your account: the email address you sign in with, the stores you manage, and sign-in records. We use it to run your account and send you the Monday email. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.
  • Email delivery: whether an email bounced or was marked as spam, so we stop sending to addresses that don't want it.
  • Cookies: the app uses only the cookies it needs to keep you signed in. This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies and loads no third-party trackers. The "your session — live" demo on the home page runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing to us.

2. Shoppers on stores that use Shop Manager

When a store uses Shop Manager, the store decides how its shoppers' data is used (the store is the controller) and Sirge handles that data only on the store's behalf and instructions (we are the processor). If you shopped on one of these stores, the store's own privacy policy applies, and the store is the right place to send requests — we help them answer.

What we collect on the store

  • Recordings of how pages are used: the page layout, scrolling, mouse movement, clicks and taps on the store's own pages, so the store can see where shoppers get stuck.
  • Visit events: pages viewed, add-to-carts, page speed, errors on the page, and where the visit came from (the referring site and campaign tags).
  • A visitor id: a random first-party cookie on the store's own domain, so two pages of the same visit, or a return visit, are counted together.
  • Orders: the order details Shopify sends the store's app (order number, value, products and time), so visits can be tied to sales.

What we never collect or do

  • We never record what shoppers type. Every input is masked in the shopper's browser before anything is sent, along with text the store marks as private and common places names and emails appear.
  • We never record checkout or payment pages, and payment buttons are blocked from recordings.
  • We strip personal details (such as email addresses) from page addresses before they are stored.
  • We don't use browser fingerprinting.
  • We don't sell or share shopper data, use it for advertising, or build profiles that follow a shopper from one store to another.

Consent

Shop Manager follows the store's Shopify cookie consent settings. Where a shopper's consent is required and hasn't been given, we don't record the visit or collect events about it; if they agree later, collection starts from that point, and it stops again if they withdraw. If a shopper's browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as an opt-out of sale and sharing and collect no advertising identifiers.

How long we keep it

  • Recordings are deleted automatically 90 days after the visit.
  • Visit events and orders are kept while the store uses Shop Manager, so it can compare this month with last. They are deleted when the store leaves (Shopify tells us when the app is removed) or asks us to delete them.

Deletion requests

When a shopper asks a store to erase their data, Shopify passes the request to us and we delete that shopper's recordings, events and order links. Stores can also ask us directly. When a store removes Shop Manager, its data is deleted.

3. Where data is kept and who helps us

Data is stored and processed in the United States, on Amazon Web Services (Oregon, us-west-2) and Supabase, and is encrypted in transit and at rest. The companies that help us run Shop Manager are listed on our sub-processors page, and how we protect data is on our security page. For stores with shoppers in the EU or UK, our data processing addendum covers international transfers.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Merchants and website visitors: email us to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. We reply within 30 days, usually much sooner.
  • Shoppers: contact the store you shopped with; they can delete your data through Shopify or by asking us.
  • Opting out: decline analytics cookies in the store's cookie banner, turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser, or clear the store's cookies.

5. Children

Shop Manager is a tool for businesses and is not aimed at children. Stores that sell to children, or sell age-restricted products, should tell us so we can agree how their store is set up.

6. Changes and contact

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell merchants by email. Questions or requests: shahz@sirge.io.
